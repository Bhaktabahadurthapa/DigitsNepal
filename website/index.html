§ १

A small dictionary of what it does.

  1. १

    live प्रत्यक्ष

    adj. — as you type

    English appears while you write: on a short pause, or the moment a sentence ends. Never mid-word. No translate button to hunt for.

  2. २

    anywhere जहाँसुकै

    adv. — in any app

    Select text in Slack, Mail, Notes or your browser and press ⌃⌥T. Read it, copy it, or replace your words in place — with undo.

  3. ३

    meaning अर्थ

    n. — word by word

    Click any word or phrase — ko barema, kura gareko thiye — for its meaning in this sentence, its literal sense, and its Devanagari spelling. Ambiguities are marked, not hidden.

  4. ४

    register शैली

    n. — six ways to say it

    Accurate, Professional, Casual, Academic, Technical, Grammar. The faithful translation always stays beside the polished one — tense, negation and certainty intact.

  5. ५

    verbatim ज्यूँका त्यूँ

    adv. — for engineers

    Kubernetes, AWS, pods, PR numbers, error codes and versions pass through untouched. If a chaina loses its “not”, you're warned.

  6. ६

    yours तपाईंको

    pron. — no account

    No sign-up, no analytics. Run a free model on your Mac, or bring a key from the AI you already pay for. Keys stay in the macOS Keychain.

§ २

In the meeting, only you see it.

Overlay mode shrinks DigitsNepal to a see-through panel that floats above Zoom, Meet or Teams. Turn on Hide in share and macOS leaves it out of screen sharing and recordings — your notes, your Nepali, your English.

  • Always on top, on every Space
  • Adjustable transparency — text stays crisp
  • One tap: copy English into the chat

Check your meeting app's share preview once; a few capture tools ignore the hide request.
